# Ingredia Screenshot Plan

Last updated: August 28, 2026

## Goal

Create App Store screenshots that show what Ingredia actually does without implying guaranteed safety.

## Visual Principles

- Show clear product results, not vague marketing claims
- Avoid wording that suggests medical certainty
- Prefer realistic example products and realistic profile settings
- Keep text large and readable
- Use screenshots that show the scan flow, result explanation, and restaurant card

## Suggested Screenshot Set

### 1. Home / Scan Entry

Purpose:
Show that the app is centered on fast scanning with a profile-aware workflow.

Suggested caption:
`Scan food products against your allergy profile`

Suggested screen:
- Home tab
- active profile visible
- main scan call-to-action visible

### 2. Product Assessment Result

Purpose:
Show the main result state and explanation of a conflict.

Suggested caption:
`See possible conflicts and what needs checking`

Suggested screen:
- Product result with `Not recommended` or `Check the product`
- findings section visible
- data confidence section visible

### 3. Better Alternatives

Purpose:
Show that the app does more than warn and can suggest more compatible options.

Suggested caption:
`Review better alternatives in the same category`

Suggested screen:
- Product result with alternatives list expanded
- source labels and confidence visible

### 4. History

Purpose:
Show that scanned products remain available locally.

Suggested caption:
`Reopen saved scans anytime from history`

Suggested screen:
- History tab
- several saved products
- visible statuses

### 5. Restaurant Card

Purpose:
Show the restaurant-use case clearly.

Suggested caption:
`Show your allergy card when ordering`

Suggested screen:
- Restaurant Card tab
- allergens visible
- cross-contamination warning enabled

### 6. Profile Setup

Purpose:
Show that users can personalize the app for their needs.

Suggested caption:
`Choose allergens, languages, and trace-warning preferences`

Suggested screen:
- Profile tab
- selected allergens visible
- reject may contain toggle visible
- language picker visible

## Devices To Prepare

Prepare at least:

- 6.7-inch iPhone screenshots
- 6.5-inch iPhone screenshots if needed by App Store Connect
- Optional smaller iPhone size if you want extra flexibility

## Capture Preparation

Before taking screenshots:

1. Create a clean demo profile.
2. Use realistic saved products in history.
3. Use one conflict example and one compatible alternative example.
4. Make sure timestamps and names look intentional.
5. Avoid placeholder or obviously broken data.

## Copy To Avoid

Do not use captions like:

- `Safe to eat`
- `Guaranteed safe`
- `Allergy-safe`
- `Trusted medical advice`

Prefer captions like:

- `Check products faster`
- `Review possible conflicts`
- `See what still needs verification`

## Recommended Demo Data Set

Use screenshots based on:

- one profile with milk and peanut allergy
- one product with direct allergen conflict
- one product with trace warning only
- one result with clear better alternatives
- one history list with mixed statuses

## Operational Note

Take screenshots on the Simulator or device only after the final user-facing strings and support information are settled, so the store assets do not drift from the shipped app.
