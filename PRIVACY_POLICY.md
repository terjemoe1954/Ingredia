# Ingredia Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

## Overview

Ingredia is an iPhone app that helps users review packaged food products against a personal allergy or intolerance profile.

This privacy policy describes what information the app uses, where that information is stored, and when data is sent to external services.

## Information Stored On Device

Ingredia stores the following information locally on the user’s device:

- allergy and intolerance selections
- profile name
- cross-contamination preference
- optional restaurant note
- selected app language
- scanned product history
- product records saved after scan or review

This information is used to personalize product assessments and keep previously viewed products available in the app.

## Camera Access

Ingredia requests camera access only to scan product barcodes.

The app does not use the camera to record video for upload or background monitoring.

## Network Requests

When a user scans a barcode, Ingredia sends the barcode to Open Food Facts to retrieve product information.

Ingredia may also send product category information in requests to search for alternative items in similar categories.

These requests are used only to:

- fetch product records
- look for similar products
- update the displayed assessment

## External Data Source

Ingredia currently uses Open Food Facts as an external product data source.

Open Food Facts is a third-party public database. Product information returned by that service can be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect.

Users should always verify the product packaging and the manufacturer’s latest information.

## Personal Data Sharing

Ingredia does not intentionally send the user’s full allergy profile, restaurant note, or scan history to Open Food Facts as named profile records.

However, the app uses the profile locally to evaluate fetched product data and to rank alternative suggestions on device.

## Analytics And Tracking

The current version of Ingredia does not include:

- advertising SDKs
- third-party analytics SDKs
- cross-app tracking

If analytics, crash reporting, or account features are added later, this policy should be updated before release of those changes.

## Data Retention

Local data remains on the device until the user deletes the app or clears that data through future app controls.

At the current stage, scan history and saved product records remain available locally for reuse inside the app.

## Children

Ingredia is not designed specifically for children under a defined age threshold, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children through account creation.

## Safety Limitation

Ingredia is a decision-support tool. It does not guarantee that a product is medically safe for any individual.
