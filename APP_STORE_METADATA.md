## Full Description

Ingredia helps you review packaged food products against your personal allergy or intolerance profile.

Scan a barcode, fetch product information, compare it with your active profile, and get a clearer view of what needs extra attention. When relevant data is available, Ingredia can also suggest better alternatives from the same or a similar category.

Ingredia is designed to help users find important product information faster, while keeping the language conservative. The app does not guarantee that a product is safe. Always verify the packaging, ingredient list, allergen labeling, trace warnings, and the manufacturer’s latest information.

### Core features

- Barcode scanning for packaged food products
- Personal allergy and intolerance profiles
- Support for cross-contamination preference
- Product assessment with clear status labels
- Explanation of detected conflicts
- Product data confidence indicator
- Better alternative suggestions when available
- Local scan history
- Restaurant allergy card
- In-app help

### Current language support

### Important limitations

- Product information depends on external database coverage
- Some products may be missing, incomplete, outdated, or incorrect
- International coverage varies by market
- Fresh product lookups and alternative search require internet access

### Safety notice

Ingredia is a decision-support tool only. It is not a substitute for food packaging, manufacturer information, or professional medical advice.
