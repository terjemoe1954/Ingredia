# Ingredia Support

Last updated: August 28, 2026

## Support Scope

Ingredia helps users scan packaged food products, compare them against a personal allergy profile, and review possible alternative items.

Support can help with:

- app usage questions
- barcode scanning issues
- product lookup failures
- language or profile setup questions
- clarification about what the app can and cannot guarantee

## Important Safety Reminder

Ingredia does not guarantee that a product is safe to eat.

Always check:

- packaging
- ingredients list
- allergen labeling
- trace warnings
- manufacturer updates

## Known Limitations

- product information depends on Open Food Facts
- database coverage varies by country
- some products may be missing
- some product records may be incomplete or outdated
- fresh lookups and alternative search require internet access

## Suggested Troubleshooting

### Scanner does not detect the barcode

- confirm camera permission is enabled
- increase lighting
- move the phone slightly closer or farther away
- make sure the barcode is fully visible
- print test barcodes at full scale when possible

### Product not found

- the product may not exist in Open Food Facts
- the barcode may scan correctly even if no product record exists
- try again later in case of temporary service issues

### Alternatives do not appear

- the product may not include enough category data
- the available candidate products may conflict with the active profile
- the network request may have failed temporarily
